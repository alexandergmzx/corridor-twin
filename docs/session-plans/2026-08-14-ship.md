### What I got wrong today, and caught

- **The camera pose came from the wrong stage.** The v1 stage and the composed
  arena both carry a mast at the same prim path, 2.1 m apart. The wrong one
  produced a full five-gate table with the violation at the same gate and a
  *better*-looking error. Two guards now refuse a cross-stage pose.
- **I blamed the estimator for the recording path.** The −10.59% looked like
  detector error until a second, independent measurement attributed −11.20% of
  it to image stamps that outrun their content.
- **I trusted a correlation I had accepted that morning.** ADR 0037's placement
  rationale had a counterexample within hours; ADR 0039 records it rather than
  editing 0037.
